A great smile starts with great habits. Here are five simple things you can do every day to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

## 1. Brush Twice a Day (the Right Way)

Use a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste. Brush for at least two minutes, covering all surfaces of your teeth. Replace your toothbrush every 3-4 months.

## 2. Don't Skip the Floss

Flossing removes plaque and food particles from between teeth where your toothbrush can't reach. Make it part of your daily routine — your gums will thank you.

## 3. Watch What You Eat

Sugary and acidic foods can erode enamel and feed harmful bacteria. Opt for crunchy fruits and vegetables, dairy products, and plenty of water.

## 4. Stay Hydrated

Water helps wash away food particles and bacteria. It also helps maintain saliva production, which is your mouth's natural defense system.

## 5. Visit Your Dentist Regularly

Even with perfect home care, professional cleanings and exams are essential. We can catch issues early and remove tartar buildup that brushing alone can't handle.

Small changes add up to big results. Start incorporating these habits today!
