Regular checkups are one of the most important things you can do for your health. Many conditions are easier to treat — and less expensive — when caught early.

## Early Detection Saves Lives

Routine exams allow your healthcare provider to spot warning signs before they become serious problems. Whether it's a cavity that hasn't started hurting yet or early signs of gum disease, catching issues early means simpler, less invasive treatments.

## Building a Relationship with Your Provider

When you visit regularly, your provider gets to know your unique health history. This makes it easier to notice changes and provide personalized recommendations.

## What to Expect

A typical checkup includes:

- A thorough examination
- Necessary screenings or X-rays
- A review of your health history
- Personalized recommendations for care

## How Often Should You Visit?

For most adults, we recommend a visit every six months. However, some patients may benefit from more frequent visits depending on their individual needs.

Don't wait until something hurts — schedule your next checkup today.
